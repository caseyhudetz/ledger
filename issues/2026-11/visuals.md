# November 2026 Visuals

Written with the visual-ideas skill. Style for all: photorealistic news photo, overcast late fall Chicago light, no identifiable faces, no legible real business signage.

## 1. Lime Scooters Migration
- **Shot:** A V-formation of green rental scooters lying on a Chicago sidewalk, all pointing south.
- **Image prompt:** Photorealistic street photo, a dozen green electric rental scooters lying on their sides in a V formation on a wide city sidewalk, all pointing the same direction, fallen leaves, overcast November sky, brick three flats, nature documentary framing, no people, no logos.
- **Shoot it yourself:** Any tipped scooter at Broadway and Belmont, shot low like wildlife photography.

## 2. Tilly's Line Reaches Skokie
- **Shot:** An endless line of people in winter coats stretching down a residential street to the horizon.
- **Image prompt:** Photorealistic wide shot, an extremely long orderly line of people in fall coats stretching down a Chicago residential sidewalk to the vanishing point, people seen from behind, a folding table water station, overcast morning, no readable signs.
- **Shoot it yourself:** The real Saturday line, from behind, with a long lens.

## 3. Stickered Garbage Can Landmark
- **Shot:** A green city trash can covered in layered stickers, with a bronze historical plaque mounted beside it and a velvet rope.
- **Image prompt:** Photorealistic close up, a Chicago public trash can completely covered in layers of colorful worn stickers, a bronze historical landmark plaque on a small post beside it, a museum style velvet rope around it, city sidewalk, overcast light, no readable brand names.
- **Shoot it yourself:** The real can, plus a printed fake plaque taped next to it.

## 4. Construction Fence Permanent Residency
- **Shot:** A chain link construction fence with a ribbon and a framed certificate zip tied to it, lost gloves on the posts.
- **Image prompt:** Photorealistic street photo, temporary chain link construction fence on a city sidewalk, a red ceremonial ribbon tied in a bow and a framed certificate zip tied to the fence, single lost gloves on the posts, orange traffic barrels nearby, overcast November light, no people.
- **Shoot it yourself:** Any long-standing Broadway fence with a printed certificate zip tied on.

## 5. Merlot Library
- **Shot:** A wine glass holding an "I Voted" sticker on a library counter.
- **Image prompt:** Photorealistic close up, an elegant red wine glass containing a single round "I Voted" sticker instead of wine, sitting on a library circulation desk, stacks of books and a date stamp in soft focus, warm interior light.
- **Shoot it yourself:** A real wine glass and sticker at home on a stack of library books.
