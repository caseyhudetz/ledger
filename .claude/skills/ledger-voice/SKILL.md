# Ledger Voice

## The feeling we want
Readers should smile and feel heard. The joke is on shared neighborhood experiences, not on individuals.

## Voice
- Deadpan news register. Write it straight, like a serious paper reporting something absurd.
- Hyperlocal. Real streets, real landmarks, real seasonal rituals (Broadway traffic, parking, rats, the lakefront, dogs, condo boards).
- Affectionate exaggeration. Take a true, recognizable frustration and push it one step past reality.
- Short headlines that land on the last word.

## Hard lines
- No libel. Never state or imply a real, named person did something they did not do.
- No insulting real people. Public officials can appear only in clearly absurd, good-natured framing.
- No real private individuals or identifiable businesses as targets.
- Punch at situations and systems, never at groups of people.

## Style
- No em dashes. No Oxford comma.
- Fake quotes come only from clearly invented residents ("local man Gary, 54").
