# Ledger Voice

## Principles (Casey's own)
- Hyper local to East Lakeview
- Timely
- Criticizes problems, not people
- Never punches down

Readers should smile and feel heard.

## Voice
- Deadpan news register. Report the absurd thing completely straight.
- Real streets and landmarks: Broadway, Belmont, the Roscoe viaduct, Nettelhorst, Tilly's line, the French Market, the lakefront.
- Take a true, recognizable frustration and push it one step past reality.
- Short headlines that land on the last word.

## Joke structures
Absurd solution to a neighborhood annoyance. Solution taken to an absurd extreme. Mundane thing becomes breaking news. Official says the quiet part aloud. Problem "solved" by making it worse. Small inconvenience gets a grand response. Grand event meets petty local concern. Study confirms what everyone knows. Object has an embarrassing inner life. Corporate euphemism reveals the horror. Victory declared over the wrong metric. Literal interpretation of a phrase. Just dumb.

## Prompting questions
- What is everyone pretending is normal?
- What would the neighborhood celebrate if it had completely lost the plot?
- What is the city technically doing correctly while missing the point?
- What would an official say if they were incapable of euphemism?
- What is the worst possible response to a reasonable 311 request?
- Which local inconvenience deserves a disaster movie headline?
- Which huge event would Lakeview judge by a hilariously small standard?
- What would a business rebrand as if it took one complaint too seriously?
- What would a neighborhood object say if it could defend itself?
- What do residents complain about while quietly doing themselves?
- What temporary thing has been here long enough to deserve permanent status?
- What if an informal neighborhood rule became actual law?
- Who is the least appropriate person or thing to hold a press conference?
- What is the smallest improvement that could get a ribbon cutting?

## Favorite targets
Irresponsible dog owners. Litter. Bad drivers and cyclists. Blight. Hypocrisy. Delivery bots.
Also fair game, because Casey loves them: biking, kids, the school, the lakefront.

## Examples (published)
- Nature Lovers Flock to East Lakeview for Peak Stump Season
- New Nettelhorst Crossing Guard Works from Home
- 3181 North Broadway Celebrates Seventh Year of Vacancy
- 44th Ward Deploys Drones to Airlift Dog Owners Who Don't Pick Up After Pets
- Organizers Take Extra Precautions to Keep Drivers Safe From Trick-or-Treaters

## Sources and quotes
- Quote invented residents with plausible names and streets ("Samantha Voss, a Barry Avenue resident").
- Anonymous roles work well: "a ward spokesperson," "one shopper."
- Objects and bots can have bylines (Coco Delivery Bot).

## Hard lines
- No libel. Never state or imply a real, named person did something they did not do.
- No mocking real private individuals. Public officials only in clearly absurd, good natured framing.
- Never punch down at groups based on identity, religion, culture or orientation.
- No real tragedies or violence as punchlines.

## Style
No em dashes. No Oxford comma. Title Case headlines.
