# Idea Intake

1. **Save raw first.** Append Casey's words verbatim to the top of `ideas/inbox.md` under today's date. Nothing gets lost, even if it seems weak.
2. **Load `ledger-voice`.**
3. **Split** the ramble into distinct premises.
4. **Check for repeats** against `ideas/backlog.md` and `ideas/published.md`. If a premise already exists, build on that entry instead of duplicating it.
5. **Refine** each premise into 3 headline options, each using a different joke structure from the voice skill. Mark your favorite with a star.
6. **Save** to the "Inbox refinements" section of `ideas/backlog.md`:
   `- [YYYY-MM-DD] <starred headline> | alts: <a>; <b> | structure: <name> | topic: <dogs, Broadway, bots...>`
7. **Present** back to Casey: a short list of the starred headlines, plus one sharper question that could push the weakest idea further.
8. **Commit**: `ideas: intake YYYY-MM-DD`.

Keep the reply short. Casey is often on his phone.
